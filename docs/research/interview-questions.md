# Interview questions

Use these open questions with savings-group members, treasurers, and chairpersons. Ask permission before recording. Do not collect passwords, account numbers, or personally identifying financial details.

1. How does your group currently record contributions and repayments?
2. How do members confirm that a contribution was received?
3. What happens when a record is disputed or corrected?
4. How are loan applications reviewed and approved?
5. Which information do different group roles need to see?
6. How are meetings and attendance recorded?
7. Where do members access the records: phone, shared computer, or paper?
8. What would make a digital record feel trustworthy and easy to verify?
9. Which languages and accessibility needs should the interface support?
